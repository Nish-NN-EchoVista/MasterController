Morpho wiring guide · which holes to solder

These are the 14 UART signals the MasterController firmware uses, marked on the two ST morpho through-hole rows along the board edges. Choose Solder side when you're working from underneath: the connectors and columns swap sides.

Source: ST UM2408 Rev 4, Table 19 (morpho pin assignment), Table 18 (Zio pin assignment), Tables 15/16 (solder bridges), Figures 4/19 (layout). Firmware pin map: MasterController.ioc.

DC4 transmit (PB13) is not on the morpho rows on a stock board. CN12 pin 30 is labelled "PB13 or PE8", and the factory default is PE8 (SB47 fitted, SB43 open; UM2408 Table 15). Use one of these:
  1. Take PB13 from the Zio header CN7, pin 5 (D18), which is marked on the diagram. No rework needed.
  2. Or rework: remove SB47 and fit SB43, then CN12 pin 30 carries PB13.

Ground

Every link needs a ground wire. Morpho GND holes: CN11 pins 8, 19, 20, 22, 49, 60, and CN12 pins 9, 20, 39, 54, 63. The nearest one is suggested per link in the table.

Before powering up: continuity check

Most of these signals also appear on a Zio header. Beep each soldered hole against its Zio pin. If they beep, orientation and pin count are right.

Keep clear of: CN12 pins 21/33 (PA9/PA10, USB OTG), CN12 pin 10 (PD8, ST-LINK VCP), CN11 pins 13/15 (PA13/PA14, SWD) and CN11 pin 7 (BOOT0). The DataController side is its USART1: F401 PA9 = TX, F401 PA10 = RX.
DC3 on CN11 pins 28/30 (PA0/PA1) has been tested on this board in both directions with a CP210x adapter at 57 600 baud. The other pins are taken from ST's manual and haven't been tested yet.
